Features

Unmatched specifications.

Loud and clear: every part of the UCE-11 Plus is engineered for cleaner, healthier water, every day.

11 Japanese plates

11 solid hybrid mesh plates from Permelec, Japan (making electrodes since 1969): titanium coated in 99.9% pure platinum. Stable oxygen levels, ORP up to −1150 mV* and fine micro-clustering, with a 15-year plate warranty and a 45+ year lifespan.

Patented SMPS PLUS power

55+ adjustable power settings keep output steady. Designed only for Tyent, it reaches strong alkaline (11.5 pH) and strong acidic (2.5 pH) water without chemicals, and protects the machine over its long life.

PAST auto-cleaning

Polarity Anti Scale Technology reverses the charge on a timer and by sensor, so minerals do not build up on the plates and hydrogen output stays high.

Turbo mode

The only ionizer that reaches 11.5 pH without chemicals, salts or enhancers, thanks to SMPS PLUS. Ideal for washing fruit and vegetables.

ECO mode

The LCD backlight switches off when the machine is idle, saving up to 99.9% of standby power.

Fixed quantity output

Pour exactly 0.5 L, 1.0 L or 1.5 L at a touch. Measured water for cooking, flasks and baby bottles.

Real-time filter indicator

The display tracks how much each filter has left and tells you when it is time to change it.

Touchscreen + jog dial faucet

A smartphone-style touchscreen and a jog dial on the faucet. Use whichever you prefer.

Customisable display

The first ionizer with a choice of display styles: full colour or night mode.

Rotates 180° and more

The faucet turns in many directions, so it suits any sink layout.

Separate outlets

Alkaline and acidic water leave through their own outlets, so they never mix.

Smart safety diagnosis

Looks after itself.

Under the sink and out of sight, so the UCE watches for problems on its own.

01

Automatic stop

Flow stops on its own to prevent overflow. You can pour again straight away.

02

Temperature sensor

Stops hot water from entering the machine by accident.

03

Raw water sensing

If the supply runs dry, the machine stops to protect the cell and power supply and to prevent leaks.

04

Filter door detection

Water stops automatically while the filter door is open.

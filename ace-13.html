ACE-13

Countertop flagship water ionizer

Thirteen plates and a full-colour touchscreen on your counter. Turn the jog dial or tap the screen, and the ACE-13 shows the pH before you pour.

  • 13platinum-coated plates
  • 7water levels*

Features

Unmatched specifications.

Loud and clear: every part of the ACE-13 is engineered for cleaner, healthier water, every day.

13 platinum plates

The most plates on any Tyent countertop machine, for strong, steady ionization.

Colour touchscreen

Pick any of seven waters and see the pH before you pour.

Jog dial

Rotate and click to choose your pH. A soft LED changes colour with the water that is flowing.

Patented SMPS PLUS power

55+ adjustable power settings keep output steady. Designed only for Tyent, it reaches strong alkaline (11.5 pH) and strong acidic (2.5 pH) water without chemicals, and protects the machine over its long life.

7 water levels

A continuous stream of seven waters, from strong alkaline 11.5 pH to strong acidic 2.5 pH, for drinking, cooking, cleaning and skin care.

PAST auto-cleaning

Polarity Anti Scale Technology reverses the charge on a timer and by sensor, so minerals do not build up on the plates and hydrogen output stays high.

Turbo mode

The only ionizer that reaches 11.5 pH without chemicals, salts or enhancers, thanks to SMPS PLUS. Ideal for washing fruit and vegetables.

Fixed quantity output

Pour exactly 0.5 L, 1.0 L or 1.5 L at a touch. Measured water for cooking, flasks and baby bottles.

Two-stage filters

Carbon and ultra-filtration stages sit behind the front panel and are easy to change.

13 plates, dipped in platinum

Inside the water cell

13 plates. Made in Japan.

Solid hybrid mesh electrodes from Permelec, Japan: titanium, coated in 99.9% pure platinum. Every plate adds 52.9 square inches of surface for the water to pass.

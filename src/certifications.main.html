Why it matters

A machine your family drinks from every day should be tested by someone else.

Anyone can print a claim on a box. Independent testing bodies check the materials that touch your water, the electrical safety of the machine, and the way it is made.

  • Materials: parts that touch water are checked for safety.
  • Electrical safety: the machine meets standards for use in your kitchen.
  • Manufacturing: consistent quality, batch after batch.

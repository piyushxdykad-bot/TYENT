Our story

Thirty years of getting water right.

Tyent began as an engineering company with a simple question: how do you make the water in an ordinary home taste better and do more? Three decades later, that question still drives every machine we build.

The beginning

Korean engineering

Tyent's first ionizers were built around solid platinum-coated titanium plates and careful power control, a standard we still keep.

Growing up

Trusted abroad

Families in the United States and other countries made Tyent one of the best-known names in water ionizers.

Design

Recognised for craft

Touchscreens, jog dials and under-counter faucets earned recognition, including Good Design Korea.

Today

At home in India

Tyent India brings the full range here, with local water testing, installation and service.

What thirty years means

The same promise, glass after glass.

Anyone can make a machine that changes pH for a few months. Making one that still pours the same water years later takes better plates, better filters and a team that comes back to check on it.

  • Built to last. Dipped platinum-coated titanium plates and automatic cleaning protect output over time.
  • Designed for real kitchens. Countertop and under-counter models in finishes that suit Indian homes.
  • Looked after locally. Water testing, installation and filter delivery by the Tyent India team.

Tyent India

The team that brings Tyent home.

Water in India changes from street to street. A flat in a metro on municipal supply, a bungalow on a borewell and a home that depends on tankers all need different care.

That is why every Tyent India installation starts with a water test in your kitchen. We recommend the right model and pre-filter for your supply, set it up, show the family how to use it, and stay in touch for filters and service.

Service network

Looked after from the first visit.

Owning a Tyent follows four simple steps. Our team handles each one, so you only have to pour.

Step 01

Water test

We check TDS and source water in your kitchen before recommending anything.

Step 02

Installation

A technician fits the machine, sets your water levels and walks the family through every setting.

Step 03

Filter reminders

The display counts litres and tells you when a filter is due. Genuine filters come to your door.

Step 04

Ongoing service

Questions, check-ups and repairs are one call away for as long as you own your Tyent.
